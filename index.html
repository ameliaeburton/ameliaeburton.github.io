About

Background

I design with the build in mind, and I build what I design.

I'm a mechanical engineering senior at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, graduating May 2027. I've designed and machined hardware for a research telescope, analyzed springs for aircraft fuel controls, and built rovers and mechanisms from CAD to finished part.

Outside engineering, I love wildlife and nature, whether that's scuba diving, shelling along the shore or traveling somewhere new.
